\chapter*{Introduction to Part IV: A Network of Portals}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Introduction to Part IV: A Network of Portals}
\label{part:network-of-portals}

This part looks beyond individual portal development toward a network of interoperable portals. It explores how emerging capabilities---agentic AI, machine-readable access, shared standards, and API-first integration---could connect portals across organizational boundaries.

Topics include agentic user experiences and access, knowledge and reasoning, learning and operational models, standards for interoperability, and machine-to-machine business communication.

\textit{An evolving vision.} This book is a continuous work in progress. Part IV gathers forward-looking ideas and experiments rather than defining a fixed delivery schedule. As proposals mature and are implemented, they are promoted to:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Part II (The Framework) --- when they affect the framework’s languages, runtime, or tooling.
  \item Part III (The Portal) --- when they shape methods, patterns, or models for portal design and delivery.
\end{itemize}

\noindent The chapter that follows, \emph{Interconnected Portals} (\cref{chap:interconnected-portals}), collects the most promising threads and defines criteria for promotion into the main body of the book.
